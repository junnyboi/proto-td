# Experimental Salvage Enemy Animations

These assets are isolated under the `experimental_salvage` namespace for prototype readability and playtesting. They were explicitly accepted from failed generation candidates and remain `placeholder: true`; their documented identity, anatomy, action, chroma, shadow, or direction defects are not MGS-approved shipping quality.

Runtime contract: 256×256 cells, eight frames per horizontal sheet, 8 FPS, bottom-center pivot, SE/NE core directions, and exact mirrored SW/NW sheets. See `provenance.json` for per-sheet hashes and accepted debt.
