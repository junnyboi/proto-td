**PASS.** The 1920×1080 image delivers the most kinetic roster fantasy. ## Comparative verdict

All four concepts meet the adult, attractive, non-explicit, original anime-gacha brief and provide genuinely different brand promises. **Concept C** has the strongest permanent loading-screen composition and premium flagship aura. **Concept B** has the strongest character-marketing and fashion appeal. **Concept A** is the safest all-purpose world-and-roster introduction. **Concept D** is the strongest action-led event visual.
