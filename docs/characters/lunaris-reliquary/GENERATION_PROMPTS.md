## Shared full-figure prompt contract

Create a premium production character design sheet for the specified clearly adult Lunaris hero. Use high-end painterly anime realism, mature anatomy and expressions, fashion-editorial ceremonial combat couture, luxurious material separation, and the exact identity, costume, palette, and silhouette from the supplied ensemble references.

Use a vertical 3:4 professional concept board on a perfectly plain pure white studio background. Show three separate, complete, non-overlapping full-body poses: one neutral identity pose, one signature-weapon combat or casting pose, and one rear or side three-quarter construction view. Include an isolated signature-weapon presentation and a small number of circular detail callouts. Render no text, labels, logos, watermarks, numbers, scenery, or additional characters. Keep every figure, limb, costume panel, hair tip, shoe, and weapon fully inside the canvas.

All characters are age 21 or older. Use confident non-explicit sensuality, mature faces, and adult body language. Reject juvenile styling, school cues, childlike proportions, explicit exposure, pornographic framing, anatomy errors, identity drift, costume drift, cropped equipment, generic weapons, or inconsistent materials.

## Shared chibi prompt contract

Translate the approved full-figure hero into a production-ready in-game chibi approximately 3.5 heads tall. Preserve mature identity markers, adult expression, hair mass, palette, costume construction, signature mechanism, and weapon. Enlarge the head, hands, feet, weapon, and key hardware for gameplay readability while simplifying micro-filigree into clear shape hierarchy.

Use the same three-pose and callout layout on a plain white 3:4 sheet. The chibi is the same adult hero, never a child or younger version. Avoid toddler proportions, baby faces, school styling, giant juvenile eyes, or cute behavior that erases the hero's established confidence.
