# Faction Visual Redesign Production Record

> **Narrative authority:** [`NARRATIVE_CANON.md`](NARRATIVE_CANON.md) is the sole binding narrative authority. This document preserves visual-development and gameplay-specialization constraints; it does not create faction lore.

The four factions are separated through silhouette grammar, material language, environmental staging, energy geometry, battlefield specialization, and heraldry. Lunaris Reliquary retains its approved adult characters, palette, costume construction, lunar mechanisms, and ritual geometry. All faction motives and uses of anima follow the Anima War canon.

## Solcrest Accord: Dawn Phalanx

Solcrest uses disciplined unity rather than mystical monumentality. The lead adult oath-marshal carries a broad sunstone shield and polearm-standard. Supporting adults include a spellblade adjudicator, a hunter with a folding beacon bow, and a ward engineer carrying interlocking shield plates. Silhouettes are upright, broad, and architectural. White-gold practical lamellar and civic heraldry separate Solcrest from Lunaris ceremonial layers.

The environment is a terraced causeway city at sunrise, with arches, aqueduct-like bridges, beacon towers, and civilian or supply columns behind a luminous formation line. Teal appears in oath-sashes, plum in seals and officer linings, black in under-armor, and warm gold in shields and dawn light. Effects use straight linked ward planes and sunrise rays, never crescents or orbital circles.

The kit rewards adjacent positions and coordinated responses. Linked units share protection, commanders rally allies, interceptors step into threatened lanes, and hunters gain bonuses against elite constructs or summoned mechanisms. Its strength is organized public defense. Its dramatic danger is the belief that a government may sacrifice some people to keep the state secure.

## Vesper Circuit: Midnight Relay

Vesper remains an information-warfare faction. The lead adult relay director wears sharply tailored midnight couture, an ivory half-mask, and a folding crossbow-antenna. Supporting adults include a wine-haired decoy artist, a shield hacker with a translucent interference fan, and a rooftop signal runner accompanied by moth-like reconnaissance drones. Silhouettes are narrow, offset, and deliberately incomplete.

The environment is a suspended night-market intelligence hub: rain-dark bridges, glowing glass kiosks, cable lifts, signal towers, and layered traffic below. Cyan filaments connect targets; wine-red interference panes split sightlines; gold is restricted to precision mechanisms. Technology uses triangles, split eyes, folded wings, and thin routing lines, not Lunaris circles or Solcrest shield planes.

The kit manipulates information and enemy intent through concealed deployment, decoys, priority marks, path rerouting, jamming, delayed traps, and mapped-defense execution. Vesper can expose PROTOS's collection routes and locate stored souls, but its methods must never imply that anima is only information. Anima is one person's real, unique soul and cannot be copied.

## Lunaris Reliquary

Lunaris retains flowing ivory and violet-black layers, brushed gold, moon-cyan gravity light, astrolabes, orbital rings, reflective planes, ceremonial symmetry, and its three approved adult launch heroes. Its equipment finds, shields, separates, and recovers souls while gravity devices control the battlefield. Visual stars and lights represent individual people, not memory copies or consumable resources.

The approved kit remains centered on soul shields and recovery devices, gravity control, elite casting, dueling, and the Moon Gate. Lunaris can return an intact soul to its living body or one prepared replacement body. Its dramatic danger is protective control: leaders may withhold souls, bodies, memories, or records in the name of safekeeping.

## Crimson Aegis: Breach Caravan

Crimson remains a mobile siege-breaking formation. The lead adult breach captain drives a segmented ram-lance through a collapsing barricade. Supporting adults include a recoil-shield bruiser, grapnel-rifle outrider, demolition medic, and shock-sail lancer deploying from a moving assault crawler. Silhouettes lean forward in a compressed wedge, with scarlet directional cloth behind blackened armor.

The environment is an active moving front of modular assault bridges, crawler treads, cable harpoons, broken fortification rings, and causeway sections locking into place under fire. Forest green marks field webbing, packs, straps, and medic identifiers. Cyan is limited to targeting lights, and weapon gold appears only on impact edges. Effects use chevrons, straight acceleration streaks, cracked rings, and directional sparks, with no violet magic or sacred geometry.

The kit turns motion into breach momentum through advance, lane changes, displacement, and attacks against fractured armor. Captains open temporary forward-deployment zones, shields convert recoil into knockback, and chained attacks escalate while the formation moves. In story presentation, Company Manus may need Crimson to hold demolition until Lunaris removes living captives and recoverable souls.

## Image-generation constraints

Generated characters must be clearly adult, age 21 or older, with mature faces, anatomy, posture, and fashion-editorial combat clothing. Ensemble concepts use cinematic 3:2 landscape composition, one clear lead, readable complete supporting silhouettes, premium painterly anime realism, and no text. Symbols use centered square composition, true transparency, generous padding, no mockup, and no lettering. Banners use portrait 2:3 composition, show the complete banner and crossbar against a neutral charcoal studio background, and contain no character, scenery, or text.

| Faction output | Required content | Exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| **Solcrest ensemble** | Oath-marshal, spellblade adjudicator, beacon-bow hunter, ward engineer, terraced sunrise causeway, straight luminous formation wards. | Moon imagery, orbital rings, ritual gowns, night-market masks, scarlet shock-sails, juvenile styling, cropped anatomy, malformed weapons. |
| **Vesper ensemble** | Relay director, decoy artist, shield hacker, signal runner, moth drones, suspended rain-dark market, cyan routes, wine-red interference panes. | Sacred temples, broad sun shields, siege charges, generic black-gold costumes, juvenile styling, cropped anatomy, unreadable gadgets. |
| **Lunaris ensemble** | Approved trio, mechanical lunar halo, astrolabe and gravity forms, individual warm-white or pale-blue rescued-soul lights. | Soul blending, duplicated people, consumable soul imagery, Solcrest rays, Vesper eyes, Crimson fracture geometry. |
| **Crimson ensemble** | Breach captain, recoil-shield bruiser, outrider, demolition medic, shock-sail lancer, crawler, modular bridge, broken fortress ring. | Violet anima magic, lunar rings, ritual gowns, stealth masks, broad sun shields, juvenile styling, cropped equipment, excessive bloom. |

Processed anima controlled by PROTOS is violet-magenta. Free or rescued souls are individual warm-white or pale-blue lights. Production reviews must also use shape, context, labeling, and value contrast so the distinction remains legible for players with color-vision differences.
