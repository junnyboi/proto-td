# Protos Faction Redesign Proposal

This proposal separates the four factions by **silhouette grammar, material language, environmental staging, energy geometry, battlefield specialization, and heraldry**. **Lunaris Reliquary remains locked**: its approved characters, palette, costume construction, sacred lunar mechanisms, and ritual identity are unchanged.

## Solcrest Accord redesign — The Dawn Phalanx

Solcrest changes from a dark ruin ensemble into a **sunlit coalition army** whose visual appeal comes from disciplined unity rather than mystical monumentality. Its lead hero is an adult oath-marshal carrying a broad sunstone shield and a polearm-standard; supporting adults include a spellblade adjudicator, a reliquary hunter with a folding beacon bow, and a ward engineer carrying interlocking shield plates. Their silhouettes are upright, broad, and architectural. White-gold armor panels create immediate separation from Lunaris because Solcrest uses practical lamellar and civic heraldry rather than flowing ivory ritual garments.

The environment is a terraced causeway city at sunrise, with restored arches, aqueduct-like bridges, beacon towers, and civilians or supply columns held safely behind a luminous formation line. Teal appears in long oath-sashes, plum in seals and officer linings, black in under-armor, and warm gold in shields and dawn light. Energy effects form **straight linked ward planes and sunrise rays**, never crescents or orbital circles.

Solcrest gameplay should reward holding adjacent positions and planning coordinated responses. Linked units share protection; commanders can rally allies; interceptors step into threatened lanes; and reliquary hunters gain bonuses against elite constructs or summoned mechanisms. Its fantasy is not passive defense but **civilization advancing behind an unbroken wall**.

## Vesper Circuit redesign — The Midnight Relay

Vesper becomes a visually unmistakable **information-warfare faction**. Its lead hero is an adult relay director in sharply tailored midnight couture with an ivory half-mask and a folding crossbow-antenna. Supporting adults include a wine-haired decoy artist projecting false silhouettes, a shield hacker carrying a translucent interference fan, and a rooftop signal runner followed by small moth-like reconnaissance drones. Their silhouettes are narrow, offset, and deliberately incomplete, with coats, masks, and weapon limbs creating broken asymmetry.

The environment is a suspended night-market intelligence hub rather than another sacred ruin: rain-dark bridges, glowing glass kiosks, cable lifts, signal towers, and layers of city traffic below. Cyan filaments connect targets across the scene; wine-red interference panes cut across sightlines; gold is limited to tiny precision mechanisms. Technology uses **triangles, split eyes, folded wings, and thin routing lines**, avoiding Lunaris circles and Solcrest broad shield planes.

Vesper gameplay should manipulate information and enemy intent. Operatives deploy from concealment, create decoys, mark priority targets, reroute pathing, jam abilities, plant delayed traps, and execute enemies whose defenses have been mapped. Its fantasy is **winning the battle before opponents understand the board**.

## Crimson Aegis redesign — The Breach Caravan

Crimson becomes a dedicated **mobile siege-breaking formation** rather than a general action roster. Its lead hero is an adult breach captain driving a segmented ram-lance through a collapsing barricade. Supporting adults include a recoil-shield bruiser, a grapnel rifle outrider, a demolition medic with compact impact charges, and a shock-sail lancer leaping from a moving assault crawler. Their silhouettes lean forward in a compressed wedge, with scarlet cloth stretched like directional sails behind blackened armor.

The environment is an active moving front: modular assault bridges, crawler treads, cable harpoons, broken fortification rings, and causeway sections locking into place under fire. Forest green is used consistently for field webbing, packs, straps, and medic identifiers; cyan is reduced to small targeting lights; weapon gold appears only on impact edges. Energy effects use **chevrons, straight acceleration streaks, cracked rings, and directional sparks**, with no violet magic or sacred geometry.

Crimson gameplay should turn motion into resource generation. Units gain breach momentum by advancing, changing lanes, displacing enemies, or striking fractured armor. Captains open temporary forward-deployment zones; shields convert recoil into knockback; and chained attacks escalate when the order keeps moving. Its fantasy is **a war machine that cannot be allowed to build speed**.

## Shared banner and symbol system

Each faction receives two standalone identity assets. The **symbol** is a simple, scalable emblem on a transparent background, designed to remain readable in one color and at small UI sizes. The **banner** is a full vertical in-world standard showing the faction palette and the same emblem through fabric construction, applique, embroidery, or metal mounting. Banners contain **no lettering**.

| Faction | Banner field | Primary emblem treatment | Secondary construction details |
