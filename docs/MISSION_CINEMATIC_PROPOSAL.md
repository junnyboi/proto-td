# Mission Intro Cinematics Proposal

**Status:** Approved for end-to-end implementation  
**Campaign:** The Anima War, Acts I–II  
**Runtime target:** Godot 4.7.2 and the existing `proto-td-web` fullscreen host

## Proposal

Every selectable campaign mission receives one **short visual prologue**. Activating an unlocked mission row in Mission Control no longer jumps straight to Field Team. It first opens a full-screen cinematic overlay, plays one canon beat lasting no more than eight seconds, and then routes to the unchanged Field Team screen. A persistent **Skip** action sits in the upper-right and supports pointer, keyboard, and controller focus. `ui_cancel` skips. Completion and skipping are presentation-only: no battle ticket, reward, save mutation, or campaign attempt exists until the later Start Battle action.

The films use only the three premium Company Manus protagonists: **Lunaris Vessel**, **Reliquary Duelist**, and **Archive Caster**. Each film is intentionally simple—one location, one protagonist action, one visible consequence—because eight seconds is a poor place to hold a committee meeting. GPT Image 2 supplies the identity-locked opening plates; Veo 3.1 animates each plate as one restrained continuous shot. Generated carrier audio supplies location ambience and the single action accent, with no dialogue.

## Experience contract

At Title, a dedicated mission-cinematic prefetch service reads the sixteen same-origin stream mappings and begins downloading in campaign order without blocking Start, Settings, or Company Command. Selecting a mission promotes that mission to the front of the queue. If the stream is ready, playback starts immediately. If it is still transferring, the approved GPT Image 2 plate remains visible with localized progress and an active Skip action. If the network is unavailable, the plate remains as the accessible fallback and the player can continue normally.

Reduced Motion never plays generated motion. It presents the same full-screen plate and a localized Continue action. The video uses cover-crop for landscape and portrait, but every hero face, signature weapon, and action is authored inside the central 42 percent. The upper-right remains intentionally quiet for the engine-rendered Skip control.

## Canon visual rules

Anima is a unique human soul, never a copied file. **Processed anima is violet-magenta and remains industrially contained. Individual free or rescued souls are separate warm-white or pale-blue lights.** PROTOS machinery uses white ceramic, black mechanisms, brushed gold, cyan functional lighting, and broken ceremonial symmetry. The protagonists retain their exact adult character sheets and signature devices; there are no substitute staffs, halos, generic swords, doubles, chibi variants, readable generated signs, subtitles, or logos.

## Acceptance criteria

All sixteen anchors must be generated by GPT Image 2 at 16:9 and pass character, weapon, palette, crop, text, and anatomy review. All sixteen final carriers must be generated by Veo 3.1 at 1080p, run no longer than eight seconds, contain one continuous shot and one deliberate hero action, preserve the upper-right Skip reserve, and contain no intelligible speech. Runtime OGV streams and extracted OGG ambience must have pinned byte sizes and SHA-256 digests. Mission selection, background prefetch, progress, fallback, Reduced Motion, Skip, completion, focus, locale switching, and unchanged save semantics must pass automated and visual tests in English and Simplified Chinese.
