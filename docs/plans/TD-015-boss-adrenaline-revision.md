## Shared battle floor

All three cues must be original instrumental mid-to-late-1990s Japanese 32-bit console-RPG lo-fi battle tracks. They share gritty 12-bit drum sampling, crunchy sampler aliasing, warm centered bass, FM/Rhodes harmonic color, restrained cassette hiss, compact early-digital room, and concise memorable motifs. Boss cues must reach battle energy in the opening seconds, retain forward rhythmic motion throughout, and use tactical contrast rather than ambient withdrawal. No section may become a calm exploration interlude. Each cue must return to an opening-compatible state for the existing four-second loop-overlap pipeline.

The score may inherit only abstract production traits from the supplied YouTube reference—dusty sampling, mellow saturation, understated melody, and late-night warmth. It must not reproduce any identifiable melody, progression, rhythm, arrangement, sound recording, or artist signature.
