## Reference-derived style contract

The supplied reference is translated into general production vocabulary, never copied compositionally:

- Mid-to-late-1990s Japanese 32-bit console-RPG and instrumental lo-fi character.
- Sparse 4–6-voice arrangements for BGM; boss cues may peak at 7–8 clearly separated voices.
- Warm centered mono FM/electric bass; jazz-colored major-7th, minor-9th, suspended, diminished-passing, and parallel-planed harmony.
- Rhodes/FM electric piano, ROMpler mallets or breathy synthesized woodwind, nylon guitar, muted digital brass, and original synthesized drum hits.
- Dry, crunchy 12-bit-style kicks/snares/rimshots or entirely original generated breakbeats; no copyrighted sample, break, melody, hook, or chord-loop interpolation.
- Restrained cassette wow/flutter, midrange warmth, faint hardware noise, mild sampler aliasing, grainy early-digital reverb, and deliberate left/center/right placement. Avoid modern vinyl-crackle clichés, sidechain pumping, glossy trap sub-bass, or contemporary cinematic trailer polish.
- Shared original five-note `wayfinder` motif: scale degrees 1–2–5–4–3 with the final two notes rhythmically delayed. It is newly composed for this score and transforms by act rather than quoting the supplied reference.

## Three-act descent

### Act I — Guild Threshold

Near-surface crypt cloisters, aqueducts, garden ruins, and expedition waystations. Emotional read: welcoming, capable, adventurous. Warm/direct light: 55–70%. Music is sun-warmed, open, tactile, and gently propulsive. Water, stone, leaves, and guild preparation are suggested by plucked guitar, Rhodes, flute, and light sampler drums rather than literal field recordings.

### Act II — Twilight Grotto

Crystal-fed undercroft, aquifers, fragmented observatory, and monumental ruins. Emotional read: wonder, archaeology, mounting threat. Warm/direct light: 25–40%. Music becomes refractive, submerged, and asymmetrical: glassy FM keys, mallets, watery degraded delays, suspended harmony, and a bass pulse that gradually darkens.

### Act III — Abyssal Vault

Obsidian vaults, chained platforms, sealed primordial architecture, and lava vents. Emotional read: oppressive, vast, climactic. Warm/direct light: 0–15%. Music is near-lightless, low-register, industrial, and immense while retaining sampler-era lo-fi intimacy. Boss music turns the pressure into a high-speed final confrontation without becoming modern trailer music.
