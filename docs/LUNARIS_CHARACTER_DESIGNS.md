# Lunaris Reliquary Launch Character Designs

The Lunaris Reliquary is the launch/loading faction. Its three ensemble anchors are the canonical starting-faction characters for visual development. Personal names remain unassigned; use the stable design IDs below in filenames, prompts, and asset manifests until narrative naming is approved.

All three heroes are clearly adult, age 21 or older, and follow [`ART_DIRECTION.md`](ART_DIRECTION.md). Their approved identity references are `Faction - Lunaris Reliquary - Refined.png` and `animations/lunaris-reliquary/lunaris-title-keyframe.png`.

## `lunaris_vessel`

### Identity

The central Lunaris vessel is a glamorous, statuesque adult woman with a mature refined face, calm commanding eye contact, and a powerful hourglass silhouette. Her very long champagne-blond hair uses a braided crown and loose flowing waves as its primary recognition shape. She is the faction's prestige flagship and must carry immediate five-star banner presence without appearing fragile, juvenile, or ornamental-only.

### Costume and palette

Her palette is ivory, violet-black, moon-cyan, and brushed gold. The costume is asymmetric ceremonial battle couture: a sculpted ivory bodice over a fitted black-violet corseted waist, one draped pale sleeve, an off-shoulder violet mantle, a split ivory-and-violet overskirt, fitted dark leggings, elegant heeled combat footwear, gold chains, crescent filigree, and circular reliquary mechanisms. The design balances exposed shoulders and fitted tailoring with regal coverage suitable for polished UI art.

### Signature weapon

Her primary weapon is the **Crescent Reliquary**, an orbiting hand-guided lunar ring focus derived from the monumental halo and circular hip mechanism in the faction artwork. It uses concentric brushed-gold rings, ivory crescent plates, a moon-cyan gravitational core, and fine constellation markings. It may hover behind her hand, contract into a hip-mounted form, or expand into a combat halo; it must never resemble generic angel wings or a mundane staff.

### Full-figure sheet contract

Show three complete full-body figures on a plain white background: a confident neutral front three-quarter pose; a controlled casting pose with the expanded Crescent Reliquary; and a graceful combat-ready rear or side three-quarter pose that reveals the braid, long hair, mantle, split overskirt, footwear, and hip mechanism. Include one isolated weapon presentation and a small set of visual callouts for the braided crown, moon-cyan core, gold mechanism, and layered costume construction. Use visual callouts without written labels if text rendering would be unreliable.

### Chibi translation

Use a production-friendly three-to-four-head-tall adult chibi. Preserve the braided crown, champagne hair mass, mature calm eyes, sculpted ivory/violet silhouette, asymmetric sleeve and mantle, gold hip mechanism, and oversized Crescent Reliquary. Keep her poised and commanding rather than cute-childlike.

## `reliquary_duelist`

### Identity

The reliquary duelist is a striking adult East Asian man with a tall athletic-muscular build, mature angular features, confident narrowed eyes, and long black hair tied in a high flowing ponytail. His exposed arms, martial posture, and elegant restraint establish physical power; his appeal is sophisticated and dangerous rather than brutish.

### Costume and palette

His palette is dark teal, violet-black, jade-cyan, and antique gold. He wears sleeveless high-collared ceremonial martial tailoring with a fitted torso, asymmetric long coat panels, dark fitted trousers, reinforced boots, broad bracers or gauntlets, arm bands, gold chains, and geometric reliquary hardware. Teal fabric and cyan energy must separate cleanly from his black hair and dark base costume.

### Signature weapon

His primary weapon is the **Jade Meridian**, a long straight reliquary spellblade with a dark metal spine, antique-gold guard and fittings, and a luminous jade-cyan energy edge or inset. The weapon should feel balanced for one-handed precision but long enough to create a decisive silhouette. Avoid oversized fantasy slabs, katanas without Lunaris mechanisms, or generic glowing swords.

### Full-figure sheet contract

Show three complete full-body figures on a plain white background: a relaxed over-the-shoulder neutral pose that preserves his loading-screen identity; a low controlled spellblade stance with the Jade Meridian fully visible; and a side or rear three-quarter pose exposing the ponytail, sleeveless tailoring, bracers, back hardware, long panels, and boots. Include one isolated weapon presentation and visual callouts for the ponytail tie, gold chain construction, bracer mechanism, and blade energy channel.

### Chibi translation

Use a broad-shouldered three-to-four-head-tall adult chibi with a mature angular expression. Preserve the long black ponytail, sleeveless teal silhouette, powerful arms, dark long panels, gold hardware, and an enlarged Jade Meridian. His stance should read as confident duelist authority, not a school-age swordsman.

## `archive_caster`

### Identity

The archive caster is a glamorous adult East Asian woman with a poised curvy silhouette, mature fine features, a knowing controlled expression, and short silver-lilac curls arranged in a soft bob with braided or pinned details. Her identity is elegant, intellectually dangerous, and ritualistically precise.

### Costume and palette

Her palette is plum-black, silver-lilac, moon-cyan, and brushed gold. She wears a sculpted black-plum ceremonial dress with a fitted bodice, asymmetric layered skirt, controlled high slit, sheer geometric sleeves, translucent cape panels, long gloves, elegant heeled combat footwear, gold jewelry, and suspended reliquary ornaments. Translucent fabrics must remain readable against white through plum edging and gold geometry.

### Signature weapon

Her primary weapon is the **Archive Astrolabe**, a hand-guided gold orbital focus with concentric rings, hanging reliquary weights, delicate chain geometry, and a moon-cyan crystal or energy point. It floats close enough to read as controlled equipment and must retain a strong circular silhouette at chibi scale. Avoid generic magic wands, books, or unexplained floating jewelry.

### Full-figure sheet contract

Show three complete full-body figures on a plain white background: a poised neutral stance with one hand guiding the compact Astrolabe; a dynamic ritual-casting pose with its rings expanded; and a side or rear three-quarter pose revealing the curled bob, translucent cape geometry, layered skirt, gloves, footwear, and suspended ornaments. Include one isolated weapon presentation and visual callouts for the hair construction, sheer-panel edging, gold ring mechanism, and moon-cyan focus.

### Chibi translation

Use an elegant three-to-four-head-tall adult chibi with a mature knowing expression. Preserve the silver-lilac curled bob, black-plum fitted silhouette, translucent edged panels, long gloves, gold jewelry, and an enlarged floating Archive Astrolabe. Keep her poised and glamorous rather than childlike.

## Shared sheet constraints

Every sheet uses a true plain white background with no environment, floor texture, frame, decorative border, watermark, or promotional logo. Figures and weapons remain fully inside the canvas. Repeated figures on one sheet must preserve identical face, body type, costume construction, hair, colors, and weapon design.

The full-figure sheets should favor tall production-board compositions with enough spacing to read three poses and one weapon presentation. The chibi sheets should show at least a neutral view, an action-ready view, and one side or rear three-quarter view, preserving silhouette and game-readability. Do not add prose labels inside generated images; the repository documentation supplies the canonical written specification.
