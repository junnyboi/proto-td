## Separation quality gate

The final concepts avoid the previous shared dependence on black-gold-cyan couture against generic floating ruins. Solcrest is now defined by **formation and sunrise civic defense**; Vesper by **information warfare and nocturnal urban routing**; locked Lunaris by **sacred lunar memory and gravity**; and Crimson by **kinetic breach momentum**. Their dominant silhouettes, environments, energy geometries, and specialization promises can be identified without reading faction names.

All concept images contain no generated text or UI. All symbols contain no lettering. All banners contain no lettering, show the full standard, expose their faction colors, and carry the corresponding symbol. Checksums are recorded in [`SHA256SUMS`](SHA256SUMS).
